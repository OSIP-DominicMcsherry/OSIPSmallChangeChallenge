You cannot meaningfully respond to what you have not first been willing to see clearly. OSIP says Seeing where you are is not the same as accepting it. You can look straight at something and still resist it, grieve it, or work to change it. Seeing does not tell you what to do. It makes it possible to respond to the actual thing rather than the version you edited for comfort. Try this Read all eleven areas below. Notice which one you keep returning to, and be honest rather than picking the one you think you should pick. One area is the whole point. could do today, tomorrow, and on the worst day of your week. OSIP says Make it almost too easy. This is not testing how much you can endure, it is testing whether you can keep a promise to yourself. If the action feels impressive, it is probably too big. Try this Write it as a specific sentence with a time attached. Not I need to exercise more, but I will walk for twenty minutes after work. Then shrink it until it is boring.
